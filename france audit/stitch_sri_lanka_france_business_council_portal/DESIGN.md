## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an authoritative, institutional bilateral presence calibrated specifically for high-level trade delegations, diplomats, and senior business executives. The aesthetic merges the gravitas of French diplomatic heritage with the formal dignity of international commerce.

### Brand Personality & Core Attributes
- **Institutional Authority:** Grounded, sober, and unshakeable. Visual weight leans heavily into established diplomatic protocol, clarity, and structural permanence.
- **Executive Readability:** Optimized for senior leadership and mature stakeholders who prioritize instantaneous legibility, crisp structural separation, and generous text hierarchies over fleeting interface trends.
- **Diplomatic Bilateralism:** Evokes the shared economic and cultural dialogue between Sri Lanka and France through restrained, symbolic color accents anchored by deep maritime and state navy.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Form
The interface adopts a **Classic Institutional Modernism** approach. It avoids superficial decorations, heavy drop shadows, or juvenile roundedness. Surfaces remain clean, bright, and architectural, using fine-drawn boundaries, structured grids, and formal typography to cultivate confidence, trust, and executive decorum.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of sovereign reliability, clarity, and visual prestige. Every color token is calibrated to ensure WCAG AAA accessibility thresholds across primary text elements, accommodating executive readership under diverse viewing environments.

### Primary Palette
- **Primary Navy (`#002654`):** The institutional anchor, reflecting maritime trade routes, state authority, and official protocol. Used for mastheads, primary action buttons, key banners, and structural navigation.
- **Deep Navy / Ink (`#0b1e3b`):** The high-contrast dark surface color, reserved for high-level executive cards, critical headers, and formal document backdrops.

### Accent Palette
- **Bilateral Crimson (`#b91c1c`):** A classic, disciplined red referencing sovereign crests and bilateral diplomatic ties. Applied sparingly to highlight critical economic alerts, formal seals, and secondary interactive accents.
- **Warm Institutional Gold (`#c59b27`):** An understated metallic accent conveying diplomatic prestige, bilateral commendations, bilateral treaty emblems, and distinguished member status.

### Background & Surface Neutrals
- **Surface Crisp White (`#ffffff`):** The foundational document canvas, delivering clean, bright contrast.
- **Surface Gallery Gray (`#f8fafc`):** An understated slate wash for structured tabular zones, document metadata ribbons, and form container backdrops.
- **Border Rule Slate (`#cbd5e1`):** A hairline boundary tone that maintains strict structural separation without visual clutter.
- **Neutral Executive Ink (`#0f172a`):** Maximum-contrast near-black for body copy and data tables to preserve effortless legibility.

## Typography

The typographic pairing provides immediate institutional dignity while accommodating mature readers through deliberate sizing, open counters, and ample leading.

### Structural Serif: Merriweather
Chosen for its substantial editorial presence, sturdy slab serifs, and exceptional legibility across digital resolutions. Merriweather confers historical weight, official permanence, and editorial authority to treaties, executive communiqués, and policy statements.

### Executive Sans: Source Sans 3
Engineered for utilitarian clarity and sustained reading comfort. With large x-height, open apertures, and unambiguous letterforms (such as distinct shapes for uppercase `I`, lowercase `l`, and numeral `1`), it eliminates reading fatigue in dense economic tables, multilateral agreements, and trade statistics.

### Typographic Rules for Readability
- **Minimum Body Scale:** Default body copy is anchored at `body-lg` (`18px`) with `28px` leading to prevent visual crowding for senior decision-makers.
- **Formal Uppercase Labels:** Sub-headers, protocol categories, and committee tags use `label-sm` or `label-md` with subtle letter spacing (`0.04em`–`0.06em`) and semibold/bold weights to reinforce editorial order.
- **Paragraph Length:** Text columns are capped at a maximum line length of 72 characters to maintain tracking rhythm and avoid eye strain.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors classical administrative printing: balanced, symmetrical, and anchored to an unyielding structural grid.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column symmetrical grid with `2rem` (`32px`) gutters and a minimum outer margin of `3rem` (`48px`). Content conforms to a fixed maximum canvas of `1280px` to maintain focused visual scanning.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) outer safe margins.

### Spatial Cadence
- Vertical pacing strictly adheres to multiples of `8px` (`0.5rem`), prioritizing breathing room over dense packing.
- Informational blocks (such as council dossiers, diplomatic dispatch summaries, and member directories) are delimited by generous padding (`space-xl` / `2.5rem`), allowing executive users to inspect one document unit at a time without cognitive clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating cards, glossy blurs, or heavy multi-layered shadows. Elevation is articulated through **tonal structural layering** and **refined architectural rules**.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Crisp White (`#ffffff`), completely flat, establishing the official paper standard.
2. **Level 1 (Sub-tier Panels & Dossiers):** Very light slate wash (`#f8fafc`) bordered by a crisp `1px` solid line in `#cbd5e1`. No drop shadow.
3. **Level 2 (Active Cards & Overlays):** `#ffffff` background bounded by a sharp `1px` border in `#94a3b8`, supported by an ultra-subtle ambient lift: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 38, 84, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(0, 38, 84, 0.03)`. The shadow is tinted with the primary navy rather than pure black to preserve tonal cohesion.
4. **Level 3 (Modal Protocols & High Dialogs):** Used exclusively for official agreements, signed resolutions, and key dialog modals. Surrounded by an institutional outline (`2px solid #002654`) and a formal shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -4px rgba(11, 30, 59, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(11, 30, 59, 0.06)`.

### Border Accents as Structural Depth
Depth is reinforced horizontally via top-border accent bands. Key council cards feature a `3px` solid top border in Primary Navy (`#002654`), Crimson (`#b91c1c`), or Warm Gold (`#c59b27`), creating a tangible, book-bound editorial feeling without spatial disorientation.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft Institutional (1)** shape language. Curves are minimal and restrained to maintain an architectural, serious demeanor suited to senior trade delegates.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Base Components (`rounded` / `0.25rem` / `4px`):** Applied to standard buttons, input fields, badges, and table headers. Corners are subtly softened to avoid digital harshness while retaining a disciplined rectangular profile.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to executive cards, member profile dossiers, and formal dialog boxes.
- **Display Shields & Medallions:** Official seals, bilateral crests, and circular diplomatic status tokens use pure circular masks (`rounded-full`), contrasted against the predominantly rectangular architecture.

## Components

Components are engineered with high touch-target standards (minimum `48px` interactive height), high visual contrast, and explicit state indicators.

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Solid `#002654` background, Crisp White text (`#ffffff`), `18px` semibold typography, `0.25rem` radius, minimum height of `48px`, horizontal padding of `24px`. Hover state: deepens to `#0b1e3b` with a crisp `2px` underline or subtle inset outline.
- **Secondary Bilateral Button:** High-contrast outlined button. Transparent background, `2px` solid `#002654` border, `#002654` text. On hover: fills with `#002654` and shifts text to `#ffffff`.
- **Crimson Action Button:** Reserved for urgent communiqués or bilateral summits: `#b91c1c` fill, `#ffffff` text. Hover shifts to `#991b1b`.
- **Focus States:** High-visibility double outline: `2px solid #ffffff` inner ring, `3px solid #c59b27` outer ring. Never hidden for keyboard or accessibility navigation.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** `48px` minimum height, `#ffffff` background, `1.5px` border in `#94a3b8`, `18px` text. Placeholder text styled in `#64748b` for immediate legibility.
- **Focused Inputs:** Border changes to `2px solid #002654` with a subtle `2px` tint ring in Warm Gold (`#c59b27` at 25% opacity).
- **Checkboxes & Radio Buttons:** Sized at `22px × 22px` (scaled up from standard `16px` for ease of physical clicking). Solid `#002654` fill with distinct white check/dot indicators when active.

### Cards & Dossiers
- Built on a crisp `#ffffff` canvas with a `1px` border in `#cbd5e1`.
- Include a formal header ribbon featuring `Merriweather` (`headline-sm`) and a `3px` top border in `#002654` or `#c59b27`.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` / `24px` to `space-xl` / `40px`) ensures documents, agenda items, and member credentials read like executive briefs.

### Chips & Protocol Badges
- **Style:** Compact, rectangular tags with `0.25rem` radius, uppercase `12px` bold lettering (`label-sm`), and generous horizontal padding (`12px`).
- **Diplomatic/Bilateral Accents:**
  - *Chamber Member:* Warm Gold tint background (`#fef9c3`), `#854d0e` text, `#ca8a04` hairline border.
  - *Trade Mission:* Navy tint background (`#e0f2fe`), `#002654` text, `#0284c7` border.
  - *Official Communiqué:* Crimson tint (`#fee2e2`), `#991b1b` text, `#b91c1c` border.

### Executive Document Lists & Data Tables
- Alternating rows using `#ffffff` and `#f8fafc`.
- Row heights minimum `56px` to permit comfortable scanning and selection.
- Headers rendered in `Source Sans 3` bold, uppercase, with a persistent `2px solid #002654` bottom border anchor.
